Case Study: Hand-Crafts

PRIMARY EXAMPLE12-Week Textile Course · Mixed Ages
Situation Weekly two-hour textile group. Two recurring problems: learners lose track of their own progress between sessions and 5–8 week drop-off — not from boredom, but from busy life.
1
Shared digital progress wall
Twelve stages from "first stitch" to "finished piece".
Each learner's card moves across the board.
Visible between sessions.
Trello · Padlet · WhatsApp
2
Photo-based skill badges
Teacher photographs each new technique mastered.
The photo becomes the learner's badge for that skill.
Collected in a shared Pinterest or WhatsApp album.
Pinterest · WhatsApp album · Canva
3
Weekly micro-quests via group chat
Monday morning prompt: "try one new stitch".
Optional, no penalty.
Photos in the group chat.
WhatsApp · Telegram · Teams
4
Digital exhibition finale
Final session: Google Slides gallery of every finished piece.
Printable certificate each learner keeps.
Google Slides · Canva
What changed for the learners
📍
Visible progress between sessions
🤝
Stronger group bond
📉
Lower drop-off mid-course
📱
Confidence with basic apps
🎓
Personal portfolio to keep
✨
Sense of finished journey
Key point: WhatsApp, Google Slides, phone camera — that is all you need.
The craft session does the work; gamification just makes progress visible.
